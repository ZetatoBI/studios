The Soul Tower

The villains you defeated don't rest. Climb their tower in your current gear: every floor is harder than the last, and every fifth floor an undying champion returns.

Choose a boon to start. Headshots heal you, and clearing a floor heals 10, or 30 if you took no damage. After each champion, camp: heal fully or take a boon. Floors 15, 30 and every 15 after grant a golden boon.

Boons last for one run. Your gear is always yours to keep.

Armory

Everything you win is kept here. Tap a piece to wear it. Win a duel to take one piece of your opponent's gear, or two for a flawless win.

Damage is bow power times the zone you hit (head ×2, body ×1, legs ×0.6), reduced by the armor on that zone. The target range always uses a standard bow.

Quick tips

Goal. Journey: knock out each duelist before they knock you out. Target range: score points with 5 arrows.

Aim. Drag up or down anywhere, then let go to shoot. The dotted line shows the start of your arc; the number beside you is your angle.

Wind. The arrow on the wind chip shows which way it blows; the number is its strength. Blowing toward the enemy carries arrows farther, so aim lower. Blowing back at you shortens them, so aim higher.

Read your misses. After each shot you'll see how far off you were, like "1.2 m short". Adjust and shoot again.

Headshots deal double damage. Legs deal less. Armor reduces damage on the part it covers.

Villagers are not your enemy. Hitting them costs a heart; lose all hearts and you lose the duel.

Camera. The button at the top right switches between wide view (see everything) and follow view (close up).

Gear. Win duels to take your opponent's gear. Three armor pieces of one material unlock a set bonus. The shield button at the bottom shows your stats.
