# Ice Crown v1.5 "Hold the Line": movement, formations and late-game scaling

Build spec for Claude Code. Read `dev/icecrown/DESIGN.md` first; every rule in its "must not regress" section still
applies. Section 6 of this spec (safeguards) is mandatory.

## 0. Problems from playtesting (why this version exists)
1. My units get stuck: workers jam at the mine entrance, and new melee soldiers get stuck behind archers when the
   army is large.
2. Big late waves shove my army backwards and I can't push back, even on Charge. I like feeling the pressure of a
   huge wave, but I want to control where the fight happens.
3. Hold and Defend feel the same, and on Hold my melee doesn't attack until an enemy touches it.
4. I want melee in front and ranged behind, without micromanaging.
5. Late waves force me to dismiss all workers and max the Forge just to survive. I want scaling beyond the current
   maximum so the late game is about growing stronger, not selling everything.

Build in this order, one commit per step: 1 movement, 2 formations and stances, 3 late-game scaling,
4 tools, balance and release.

---

## 1. Movement and collisions
Goal: my own units never get stuck on each other, on workers or on buildings. Collisions with enemies stay,
because the front line is the heart of the fight.

1. **Allies pass through allies.** Friendly units (soldiers, heroes, pets, workers) ignore collisions with each
   other while either one is moving or fighting. Only when both are standing still, apply a gentle spread
   (about 30% of the current strength) so an idle army doesn't collapse into one pile.
2. **Workers are ghosts.** Workers ignore all unit collisions (friendly and enemy) and never block anyone.
   The mine entrance and the Town Center drop-off can hold any number of workers at once.
3. **Mass decides who gets pushed.** Replace the equal split in friendly-versus-enemy separation with mass:
   the lighter side moves more. Starting masses (put them in a table):
   - enemy 1, troll or knight 2.5, boss 12;
   - soldier 1.5, heavy unit 2.5, hero 2;
   - multipliers by stance, applied to my units: Hold ×3 when standing in formation (bracing), Charge ×2 while
     advancing, Defend ×1.
   Result: a braced line holds against a normal wave, a charge pushes enemies back, and only a much bigger or
   heavier wave (or a boss) can shove the line. The push-back feeling stays, but it's earned by the enemy.
4. **Steer around buildings instead of bumping into them.** If a unit's straight path crosses a building (or the
   mine), add a sideways steering force to go around it.
5. **Anti-stuck safety net.** If any friendly unit has moved less than 4 world units in the last 1.2s while trying
   to reach a point more than 15 away, it ignores all collisions for 1.5s. Log a debug counter so the balance
   runner can report how often this fires (it should be rare after points 1 to 4).

## 2. Formations and stances
### 2.1 Formation around the banner
- The banner has a **facing**: towards where the enemy comes from (the nearest upstream point of the map's road
  from the banner). Recompute when the banner moves.
- Slots are laid out in rows perpendicular to the facing, up to 8 per row:
  **front rows: melee and heavy; behind them: ranged and healers; behind those: siege.**
  Heroes stand at the ends of the front row unless the player has moved them away.
- New units walk to their slot. Because allies pass through each other (step 1), they never get stuck behind the
  back row.
- While dragging the banner, show faint slot markers (melee, ranged, siege in different colours) at the drop point,
  rotated to the new facing.

### 2.2 Stances (clear, different jobs)
Rename the stances and make each one do a clearly different thing. The stance button shows the icon and name;
the first time the player taps it, show a small card explaining all three (once, remembered in `icecrown.prog`).

| Stance | What the army does | Mass | Banner drag |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hold the line** | Stay in formation. Each melee soldier has a **guard radius of 45** around its slot: it steps out to attack any enemy inside that radius, then returns. Ranged fire at anything in range. Nobody chases further. | ×3 when in formation | March to the new spot, ignoring enemies unless attacked |
| **Defend** (default) | Stay in formation until an enemy enters the defence zone (radius 170 around the banner). Then every unit fights freely inside the zone, and returns to formation when it's clear. | ×1 | Same as Hold |
| **Charge** | **Fight your way to the banner.** The army advances towards the banner position in formation, attacking every enemy it meets on the way (attack-move), and keeps pushing forward. Once it reaches the banner it holds there, fighting anything within 120. | ×2 while advancing | Dragging the banner sets where the army pushes to |

This gives full control of where the fight happens: drag the banner up the road on Charge to push a wave back;
switch to Hold the line to brace against a massive wave; use Defend for the default "fight around the base".
Fix the current bug where melee on Hold never attacks unless touched.

Show the defence zone (Defend) or guard radii (Hold) briefly on stance change, as now. For Charge, show an arrow
from the army to the banner.

## 3. Late-game scaling
The late waves should push the player to **grow**, not to strip their base.

1. **Workers cost no food.** Food is only for the army and heroes. (Late waves were forcing players to dismiss
   workers. This removes that trade-off.)
2. **Castle: a third Town Center tier after the Keep.** Cost 650 gold and 450 lumber, 30s to build. Gives +1000
   health, +15 food, stronger Town Center arrows, a taller building with a second tower and banners.
   Unlocks: Forge levels 6 to 8, the siege unit, and Masterwork.
3. **Forge levels 6 to 8** for Weapons, Armor, Tools and Hero gear, requiring the Castle. Same effect per level as
   now; cost grows ×1.45 per level from level 5 (not ×1.6), so it stays reachable.
4. **Masterwork (endless).** Once Weapons and Armor are both at level 8: a repeatable research. Each purchase gives
   soldiers and heroes +4% damage and +4% health. Cost starts at 600 gold and 400 lumber and rises 25% per purchase.
   Show the Masterwork count on the Forge.
5. **Veterancy.** Soldiers earn ranks from kills (the final blow counts): rank 1 at 6 kills, rank 2 at 15,
   rank 3 at 30. Each rank: +12% health and damage, and 50% faster healing between waves. Draw 1 to 3 small gold
   chevrons above ranked soldiers. Keeping your army alive now matters.
6. **Two new units:**
   - **Cleric** (Kingdom) / **Witch Doctor** (Horde). Needs the Keep. 90 gold, 60 lumber, 2 food, 110 health.
     Weak attack; every 1.5s heals the most injured ally within 110 by 18 (scales with Weapons). Stands in the
     ranged rows. Draw with a robe and a glowing staff (reuse the Mage drawing, different colours).
   - **Trebuchet** (Kingdom) / **Catapult** (Horde). Needs the Castle. 200 gold, 150 lumber, 4 food, 260 health,
     speed 30. Every 3.2s hurls a stone that hits a radius of 55 for 60 damage, range 240, but can't hit enemies
     closer than 60. Answers huge dense waves. Stands in the back rows. Draw as a wooden siege engine.
7. Update the Army and Forge panels, the ring menus, thumbnails and the How to play list for all of the above.

## 4. Tools and polish
1. **Seeded runs.** When the URL has `?debug&seed=N`, replace `Math.random` with a seeded generator (mulberry32 is
   already in the code) at startup, so balance runs are reproducible and configurations can be compared with fewer
   runs. Add a `--seed` option to `run-balance.mjs` (default: a fixed seed per run index).
2. **Fix the bot's gold starvation.** Rebalance worker jobs on every think (target 60% gold while the mine has gold),
   not only for idle workers. This removes the known early loss on Silver.
3. **Teach the bot the new systems**, so balance numbers are meaningful: it upgrades to Castle after the Keep when
   affordable, trains a healer for every 6 soldiers and a siege unit for every 10 once available, buys Forge levels
   up to 8, then Masterwork with spare gold. It uses the Defend stance. Report the anti-stuck counter.

## 6. Safeguards (mandatory)
**Protect the work**
- After step 1's commit, push the branch and open the pull request as a **draft**, not merged. Push after every
  later commit, so nothing is lost if the session stops.

**Time and iteration limits for balance**
- After steps 1, 2 and 3: one quick seeded run only (1 run per map, all 4 maps). No tuning yet unless something is
  badly broken (a map lost before its halfway wave, or page errors).
- Tuning happens once, after step 3: **at most 3 rounds**, each round at most 4 configurations, quick seeded runs
  only. No per-class or per-talent isolation studies unless a target is missed by more than half after round 2.
- If targets aren't met after 3 rounds, **stop tuning**, keep the best configuration found, and report where each
  map landed. Never start a 4th round.
- Final: one full run (2 seeded runs per map). Total time spent on balance runs should stay under about 25 minutes;
  if it goes over, stop and report.
- Don't leave background tasks running unattended; if any command runs longer than 10 minutes, stop it and use a
  smaller run.

**Scope**
- Only change files in `games/icecrown/` and `dev/icecrown/`.
- If something in the spec is ambiguous, pick the simplest option that fits DESIGN.md, and list it in the PR.
- Keep save compatibility: the run save becomes `v: 3` (adds Castle tier, Masterwork count, veterancy per unit,
  stance and banner position); `v: 1` and `v: 2` saves must still load.
