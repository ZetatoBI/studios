# Ice Crown v1.4 "The Long Night": story and heroes

Build spec for Claude Code. Read `dev/icecrown/DESIGN.md` first: every rule in its section 2 still applies
(map taps never spend, tap-twice for costly or irreversible actions, silent 1.5s logo, silent updates, no ads).

## 0. Goals and scope
Give the campaign a story the player wants to see through, and make heroes feel personal from run to run.
Four features, built in this order, one commit each:
1. Story cards and boss dialogue
2. Hero talents
3. Boss gear (Armory)
4. Chronicle, polish, balance and release

Out of scope for v1.4: new maps, new art pipeline, pathfinding, backend. Don't split `index.html` into modules in
this version.

---

## 1. Story

### 1.1 The arc
A thousand winters ago the first Wardens sealed Hrimgar, the Pale King, beneath the Ice Crown at the roof of the
world. Now the seal is cracking. Rifts are tearing open across the North, and Hrimgar's four servants lead the raids:
Skarn the Troll King, Grimhorn the Frost Giant, Vyrnax the Frost Wyrm, and finally Hrimgar himself.
The player is the new Warden. The seal can be remade only by breaking the four "hearts of winter", one per servant.

**Narrator:** Maera, an old frost-seer who travels with the Warden. She speaks every story card.
Tone: warm, weathered, a little wry. Short sentences. Never more than about 60 words per card.
Both races share the same story; use the race's hero names (`S.R.heroNames`) where a line mentions a hero.

### 1.3 Boss dialogue (in battle, never blocking)
Show as a speech strip at the top of the stage (under the boss bar) for about 3 seconds, with the boss's name,
plus a short floating line above the boss. Play the existing boss sounds as now.

| Boss | On arrival | At enrage (below 30%) | On death |
|---|---|---|---|
| Skarn | "Little fire-keepers. Skarn will eat your ferry, and your fire." | "SKARN DOES NOT FALL!" | "The Crown... promised... no spring..." |
| Grimhorn | "I held this gate for three hundred years. Now I hold it for him." | "The mountain is MINE!" | "Free... at last. Tell the dwarves... I'm sorry." |
| Vyrnax | "Your fires are small. Let me show you true cold." | "Burn in the frost!" | "The sky... is yours. For now." |
| Hrimgar | "A thousand winters I have waited. You are one small candle." | "Kneel, and be frozen forever!" | "The crown... cracks... The night... ends..." |

When a boss appears for the second time or later in a campaign (for example Skarn on Hollow Pass), use a
"return" arrival line instead:
Skarn "Skarn is BACK, fire-keeper.", Grimhorn "Again, Warden. Always again.", Vyrnax "The storm returns.",
Hrimgar has no return line (he only appears once).

### 1.4 Card visuals
Build each card from existing drawing code, no image files:
- Background: the map's painted preview (`mapPreview`), darkened, with slow drifting snow.
- Speaker portrait: Maera drawn with `drawFigure` (hooded robe, staff with a pale blue orb, grey hair), about
  3x scale, bottom-left. For boss lines on cards, use the boss thumbnail enlarged.
- Text panel: bottom third, Cinzel speaker name, Alegreya Sans body, generous line height, fits a 360px-wide phone
  without scrolling.
- Transitions: 250ms fade. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### 1.5 When cards appear
- Intro cards: after "March" on the briefing, before the battle starts. The game is paused while a card is up.
  Show them the first time a map is started; on replays show them only if the player taps "Story" on the briefing.
- Mid lines: as the non-blocking speech strip (not a full card) at the listed moment.
- Victory card: before the existing Victory modal. Epilogue after the Gold victory card, first time only.
- Defeat line: as the first paragraph of the existing defeat modal (replaces the generic sentence).
- Continuing a saved run never shows intro cards again.

## 2. Hero talents
At levels 3, 6 and 9 each hero chooses one of two talents. Choices are permanent for that run (reset each battle,
like levels).

### 2.1 Flow (must not interrupt combat)
- When a hero reaches 3, 6 or 9, their portrait in the hero bar gets a glowing gold "!" badge and a short toast:
  "{hero} can learn a talent".
- Tapping that portrait (without dragging) opens the talent picker: two big cards side by side with icon, name
  and one-line effect. Picking uses **tap-twice** like other permanent choices. A "Later" button closes it.
- The game keeps running while the picker is open (it's small and can be ignored). Unpicked talents wait.
- The Army tab shows each hero's chosen talents.

### 2.2 Talents
Numbers are starting values; keep them in a `TALENTS` data table next to `HEROES` so they're easy to tune.

**Warrior**
- L3: *Iron Wall*: Taunt also gives soldiers within 120 +3 armor for 4s. / *Bloodied Blade*: Cleave heals the
  Warrior for 25% of the damage it deals.
- L6: *Rallying Presence*: soldiers within 140 attack 12% faster (passive aura, show a faint gold ring). /
  *Unbreakable*: once per wave, dropping below 20% health makes him immune to damage for 3s.
- L9: *Champion*: +25% health and +15% damage. / *Warlord*: War Cry cooldown -40% and it also gives +20% move speed.

**Mage**
- L3: *Wildfire*: Fireball leaves burning ground for 3s (radius 45, 12 damage/s, orange flames). /
  *Deep Freeze*: Frost Nova's slow lasts 6s, and non-boss enemies are frozen in place for the first 1s.
- L6: *Arcane Focus*: all her cooldowns -15%. / *Chain Bolt*: basic attacks jump to a second enemy within 80 for 50%.
- L9: *Twin Meteors*: Meteor strikes twice, 0.6s apart, the second at the next-biggest crowd. /
  *Winter's Heart*: Frost Nova radius +40% and cooldown -30%.

**Ranger**
- L3: *Predator*: Hunter's Mark also slows the target by 30%. / *Quick Draw*: +25% attack speed.
- L6: *Rain of Arrows*: Volley fires 50% more arrows. / *Eagle Eye*: +35 range, and Hunter's Mark lasts 9s.
- L9: *Deadeye*: Snipe cooldown -40%, and Snipe applies Hunter's Mark. / *Pack Leader*: two wolf companions fight
  beside her (use the Frost Wolf drawing recoloured grey-brown with a blue collar; each about 40% of a Footman's
  strength; they respawn at the start of each wave if killed; they count as army units for stance and banner, but
  cost no food).

Autocast rules stay the same. Talents that change abilities must also work when the ability is autocast.

## 3. Boss gear (Armory)
Each boss drops one item the **first** time the player defeats that boss, on any map, win or lose.
Items are permanent (saved in `icecrown.prog`) and equipped between battles, one item per hero class.

| Item | From | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Skarn's Tusk Charm | Skarn | Regenerates 1% of max health per second |
| Grimhorn's Stoneheart | Grimhorn | +20% health and +2 armor |
| Vyrnax's Frost Scale | Vyrnax | Takes 30% less damage from projectiles and breath; can't be slowed |
| Shard of the Pale Crown | Hrimgar | +20% ability power and -10% cooldowns |

- When a boss drops its item: a gold banner "Item found: {item}" and a short fanfare, at the moment of the kill.
- **Armory screen:** a new button on the battle list (next to the Crowns button) and on the briefing. It shows the
  three hero classes, each with one slot; tap a slot, then tap an owned item to equip it (tap-twice not needed,
  equipping is free and reversible). Unowned items show as locked silhouettes with "Defeat {boss}".
- Item art: draw small icons in code (a tusk on a cord, a grey stone heart, a pale blue scale, a crown shard).
- Gear applies through `applyStats` and the damage and ability code, so it works with saves and continues.

## 5. Data and compatibility
- `icecrown.prog` gains `story` (ids of cards seen), `gear` ({owned: [...], equipped: {warrior, mage, ranger}}) and
  `storyOn`. Old saves without these fields must load with safe defaults.
- `icecrown.save` (the run save) becomes `v: 2` and adds each hero's talents. A `v: 1` save must still load
  (heroes simply have no talents yet, and pending talent badges appear for their current level).
- No other storage key or the manifest `id` may change.

## 6. Balance
Talents and gear make heroes stronger, so re-run the bot on all maps (2 runs each) after step 3 and again at the end.
- The "no upgrades" and "some upgrades" profiles must stay within the targets in DESIGN.md section 4.
- With "upgrades + gear", Gold should be winnable but tight (the bot wins at least one of two runs).
- If results drift, tune **only** the map knobs (`gm`, `hm`, `lateK`) or the talent numbers, never the fixes listed
  in DESIGN.md 4 "Root causes". Report before-and-after numbers.
- Check hero damage share in a big late wave stays well under 70% with talents and gear.
