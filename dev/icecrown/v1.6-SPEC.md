# Ice Crown v1.6 "The Ember Deep": the saga, the campaign layer, the Dwarves, Campaign 2

Build spec for Claude Code. Read `dev/icecrown/DESIGN.md` and `dev/STANDARDS.md` first; every "must not regress"
rule still applies. Section 9 (safeguards) is mandatory.

## 0. What and why
The owner finished the first arc and enjoyed it, but after four battles the game feels over. v1.6 turns Ice Crown
into a saga of four campaigns, adds decisions that carry across battles, and ships the second campaign in full.

Build in this order, one commit per step:
1. Saga framework (campaign select, data model, saves)
2. Campaign layer (heroes carry over, Spoils of War boons)
3. The Dwarves (new playable race)
4. The Ember Legion (new enemy faction, bosses, hazards) and the volcanic look
5. Campaign 2's four maps, story and unlocks
6. Balance, docs and release

---

## 1. The saga: "The Shattered Crown"
When Hrimgar fell, the Ice Crown shattered. Four shards flew across the world like falling stars; each corrupts a
realm and wakes its monsters. Vael, the Shard-Witch, is collecting them to forge a new crown.

## 2. Campaign layer
### 2.1 Heroes carry over within a campaign
- Each campaign remembers each hero class's level, XP and talent picks (`prog.camp[c].heroes`).
- When a hero is recruited in a battle of that campaign, they arrive at their remembered level with their talents.
  Pending talent choices still show the gold "!" badge.
- After every battle (win or loss), save the heroes' current level, XP and talents back to the campaign.
- Heroes reset only if the player chooses "Restart campaign" (a button on the campaign's battle list, tap-twice,
  which also clears that campaign's boons but keeps stars and Crowns).
- Campaign 1 starts with empty campaign state for existing players.

### 2.2 Spoils of War (boons)
After the **first** victory of each of a campaign's first three battles, the player picks 1 of 3 boons that last
for the rest of that campaign. The three offered are chosen from the pool with a seeded random (campaign id plus
battle index), so a player sees the same offer if they reload. Show them as three cards with an icon, a name and a
one-line effect; picking uses tap-twice. Owned boons show as small icons on the battle list and in the in-battle menu.

| Boon | Effect |
|---|---|
| Quartermaster | Start each battle with +150 gold |
| Stonemasons | Buildings construct 40% faster |
| Sharpened Steel | Soldiers +10% damage |
| Thick Hides | Soldiers +12% health |
| Eagle Towers | Towers +20% range |
| Field Medics | Soldiers and heroes heal 1% of max health per second, even in combat |
| Veteran Recruits | New soldiers start at rank 1 |
| Rich Seams | Mines hold 25% more gold; workers carry 10% more |
| War Drums | The army moves 15% faster |
| Hero's Oath | Heroes +10% health and ability power |
| Iron Walls | Town Center +30% health; its arrows deal 50% more damage |
| Bounty Hunters | +30% gold from kills |

Completing a campaign's final battle for the first time instead gives a **campaign reward** (section 5.3).

## 3. The Ironhold Dwarves (new playable race, Campaign 2)
Slow, stubborn, armoured, masters of stone and powder.

- **Perks:** buildings +25% health; towers +15% range; workers carry 10% more gold; soldiers +2 armor but move
  10% slower.
- **Names:** worker Miner, melee Shieldbearer, ranged Thunderer, heavy Ironclad, healer Runepriest,
  siege Bombard. Heroes: Thane Borin (Warrior), Runesmith Hilda (Mage), Kara Ironshot (Ranger).
- **Look:** shorter and stockier than humans (about 0.9 height, 1.15 width); big braided beards (new `beard` head
  option, colour varies per unit: brown, red, grey); horned or crested helms; bronze and steel metals.
  Thunderers carry long handguns: a muzzle flash and a small smoke puff on each shot, with a sharp "crack" sound.
  The Bombard is a stubby bronze cannon on a wooden carriage.
- **Buildings:** same footprints as now, with a dwarven palette: dark granite walls, copper and bronze roofs,
  softly glowing cyan rune lines along the trim, forge chimneys with ember sparks, red and gold banners.
- Dwarves are fixed for Campaign 2. After Campaign 2 is completed, they become selectable for Campaign 1 too
  (that's the campaign reward, section 5.3).

## 4. The Ember Legion (new enemy faction) and the volcanic look
### 4.1 Enemies
Built on the existing enemy behaviours, with their own looks, names and twists. Keep all numbers in the `ENEMIES`
table; starting stats as listed relative to the Frost equivalents.

| Enemy | Based on | Twist |
|---|---|---|
| Magma Hound | Frost Wolf | Fast; leaves a short burning trail (6 damage/s for 1s) |
| Fire Imp | Rime Goblin | Fast, weak, comes in big swarms |
| Imp Slinger | Goblin Archer | Throws small fireballs (splash radius 20) |
| Ash Walker | Frozen Dead | Armored |
| Flamecaller | Goblin Shaman | Instead of healing: nearby raiders get +20% speed and damage for 5s, every 6s. Kill it first. |
| Magma Golem | Ice Troll | No regeneration; splits into 2 Ember Blobs on death |
| Ember Blob | (new, small) | 80 health, fast, weak hits |
| Obsidian Knight | Frozen Knight | Heavily armored elite |

### 4.2 Bosses (telegraph, half-health special and enrage, as existing bosses)
| Boss | Based on | Special |
|---|---|---|
| Pyrrhus the Imp King | Skarn | Summons 6 Fire Imps at half health |
| Gorgath the Molten Colossus | Grimhorn | His slam leaves a lava pool (radius 60, 20 damage/s for 4s) |
| Ashgar the Cinder Drake | Vyrnax | Flies; fire breath leaves burning ground in the cone for 3s |
| Kragmor, the Mountain's Heart | Hrimgar | Slam, summons 2 Magma Golems at half health, and calls an eruption (section 4.3) at half health and when enraged |

### 4.3 Hazards (new)
Telegraphed, fair, and they hurt **both sides**, so positioning and stances matter.
- **Lava vents** (Deep Forges): 5 vents marked on the ground. Every 45s two random vents glow orange for 3s, then
  erupt: 80 damage in radius 50 to everyone, plus a burst of embers and a deep rumble.
- **Ash storms** (Ashen Halls): every 60s for 12s, ash fills the halls: ranged units and towers lose 25% range,
  lighting dims. Banner "Ash storm" when it starts, as with blizzards.
- **Eruption** (Heart of the Mountain): every 5th wave, and when Kragmor calls it, 10 red circles appear across the
  base for 1.5s, then burning stones land: 60 damage in radius 40 (buildings take half). Screen shake, rumble.
- Show hazard warnings to the player in the wave preview and the briefing ("Lava vents erupt every 45 seconds").

### 4.4 The volcanic look
A new theme set for Campaign 2 maps:
- **Ground:** dark basalt and ash grey with cracks that glow faintly; glowing lava rivers with slowly moving
  bright streaks; obsidian spires and rubble at the edges; rising ember particles everywhere instead of snow.
- **Lumber:** giant glowing fungus instead of pine trees (stems plus caps in teal and violet, with spots), chopped
  the same way.
- **The rift:** a fiery crack with an orange glow and a heat shimmer, instead of the frost portal.
- **Lighting:** Ashen Halls and Heart of the Mountain use the night-lighting system with warm light sources (lava,
  forges, fires, spells).
- **Music:** reuse the music engine with a darker, lower variant for Campaign 2 (deeper drones, anvil-like
  percussion, a slower boss theme). No new audio files.

## 6. Balance
- Campaign 1 targets (DESIGN.md) still apply for the fresh-start profiles. Add a **campaign profile** to the bot:
  it plays a campaign's four battles in order, carrying heroes and picking the first boon offered each time.
  With carry-over, Campaign 1 may get easier, but on The Ice Crown the bot must still finish with the Town Center
  below 80%.
- Campaign 2 targets with the campaign profile and "some upgrades":
  Emberfall Gate wins, 5 to 15 soldiers lost; Deep Forges wins, 20 to 35 lost; Ashen Halls wins but costly
  (35 to 55 lost) or loses in its last 3 waves; Heart of the Mountain wins in at most 1 of 2 runs, and without
  upgrades falls before wave 22.
- The bot must not stand in telegraphed hazards on purpose, but needs no special dodging logic; hazards should be
  survivable without micro. If the bot loses mostly to hazards, reduce hazard damage before touching wave knobs.
- Tune only: map knobs, new enemy and boss numbers, hazard numbers, boon and gear numbers.

## 9. Safeguards (mandatory)
- Commit after each step. After step 1, push and open a **draft** PR (not merged); push after every commit.
- Balance: after steps 2, 4 and 5, one quick seeded run only (1 run per map, the maps that changed). Tuning only
  in step 6: at most 3 rounds, at most 4 configurations each, quick seeded runs. No isolation studies unless a
  target misses by more than half after round 2. Final: one full run (2 seeded runs per map, both campaigns).
  Keep total balance time under about 30 minutes; if over, stop tuning and report.
- If any command runs over 10 minutes, stop it. If the whole job passes about 2.5 hours, stop at the end of the
  current step, push, and report what's left.
- Only change `games/icecrown/` and `dev/icecrown/`. No changes to other games or the Studios page.
- If something is ambiguous, choose the simplest option consistent with DESIGN.md and list it in the PR.
- Before marking the PR ready: headless check at 360x640 with no page errors; screenshots of the saga screen, a
  Dwarf army in formation, a lava vent warning, and Kragmor. Then mark the PR ready for review. Do not merge it.
